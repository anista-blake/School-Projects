Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions

What is Bloom Balance?
Bloom Balance is a health and wellness web application designed to help users track their menstrual cycles, mood, energy levels, and find healthy recipes.
Who is the target audience for Bloom Balance?
Bloom Balance is primarily designed for Women, but it can be used by anyone.
Is Bloom Balance free to use?
Yes, Bloom Balance is completely free to use.
How can I create an account?
You can create an account by clicking on the "Sign Up" button on the homepage and filling out the registration form.

If you have any other questions, please feel free to contact us.
